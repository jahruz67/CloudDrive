PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Your files never become our files.

CloudDrive connects your phone directly to your Nextcloud server. There is no CloudDrive account, relay, analytics pipeline, or third-party storage.

  • ✓
    Credentials stay encryptedYour app password is protected by Android Keystore.
  • ✓
    No broad storage permissionAccess is handled through Android's secure system APIs.
  • ✓
    Open source, end to endInspect every request and build the app yourself.
